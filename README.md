MVP App: 
Medication Reminder

Purpose:
To ensure patients take their medications on timely basis.
To worsening their medical conditions.

Features:
Allows set up of medicines frequencies and timings
Based on pre-selected timings, reminders are triggered
App is compatible with Android and iOS mobile phones
Once patient had taken the meds, users need to acknowledge “Taken” in app to stop reminder for that instance. Else, the app will continue to send reminders

Future phases:
Extend use of app to caregivers
Dashboard for caregivers to monitor patients intake of meds
Also trigger alarms to caregivers if patients missed their meds doses
